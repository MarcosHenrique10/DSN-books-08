“I am the Ghost of Christmas Yet to Come,” the Spirit bellowed. “Who are you looking for in this lonely place?”

“Please, sir,” begged Scrooge, “help me find Tiny Tim.”

The Spirit pointed, and Scrooge saw the Cratchits gathered around a tombstone. Bob Cratchit laid Tiny Tim’s crutch on the grave, tears rolling down his face.

Scrooge felt awful and wanted to tell the Cratchits how sorry he was, but then the church bells began to toll once more.
