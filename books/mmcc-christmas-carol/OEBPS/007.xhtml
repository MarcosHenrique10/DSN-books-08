Inside Scrooge’s office, his assistant, Bob Cratchit, was hard at work. But then he realized that it was so cold in the office, the ink for his quill had frozen! “Surely,” Cratchit thought, “Mr. Scrooge won’t miss just one piece of coal . . .”

But just as Cratchit opened the furnace to add the coal, Scrooge burst through the door. “Caught you!” he barked. “That coal costs a pretty penny, you know, and you just used a piece last week. Now get back to work!”
