Scrooge stepped back from the window to see that the Spirit of Christmas Present had disappeared, and a smoky haze hung in the air.

“Help!” he cried into the smoke. “Don’t leave me here!”

The smoke grew thicker and flew up his nose, and he sneezed so hard that he went flying through the air, landing with a thud on a pile of snow.

As the smoke cleared, Scrooge realized he was now in a graveyard . . . and he was no longer alone! A horrible specter stood before him.
