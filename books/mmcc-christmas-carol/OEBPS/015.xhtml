Scrooge locked up the office and headed home. But when he got to his door, he saw that his door knocker had changed into the face of his old business partner, Jacob Marley!

“Scrooooooge!” the knocker called.

Frightened, Scrooge yanked open the door and ran for the stairs. Behind him, he heard the sound of chains clanking, and he flew up the stairs three at a time!
