CHRISTMAS EVE LONG AGO,

the townsfolk of London hurried home through the snow, carrying gifts.

But not Ebenezer Scrooge. He ignored the pretty snow and gaily decorated houses. He rushed past a group of carolers, muttering, “Bah! Humbug!” And when they asked him for a coin to feed the poor, he replied, “If the poor want to be rich, let them work as hard as I do!”
