And then another bell tolled, and Scrooge opened his eyes and shouted with joy. He was in his own bed!

Scrooge jumped up and ran to the window. The church bells were ringing because it was Christmas morning!

“But if it’s just Christmas morning,” Scrooge thought, “then what the Spirit showed me last night has not yet happened. Tiny Tim is alive! There’s not a moment to lose!”

He threw on his hat and coat and ran outside.
