“Surely not on Christmas Day, Mr. Scrooge,” Mrs. Cratchit pleaded.

Just then, Tiny Tim walked by the door and called out to his father, “Oh, look! Is that a teddy bear in the bag?”

Sure enough, there was a teddy bear poking its head out! Scrooge, still scowling at Bob Cratchit, swung the large sack around and threw it on the floor.
