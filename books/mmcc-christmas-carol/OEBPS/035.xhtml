The Cratchits sat around their table, ready to carve up the smallest goose Scrooge had ever seen. Then a small boy hobbled into the room, leaning on a crutch.

“Who is that boy?” Scrooge asked.

“That is Tiny Tim,” replied the Spirit. “He will soon be one less mouth for the family to feed. He is very sick and must have good food every day to make him well.”
