Scrooge was determined to ignore what he had seen. He quickly got ready for bed and pulled the covers right up to his nose. But just as he was finally ready to fall asleep, a little figure appeared from the shadows, wearing a top hat and carrying an umbrella.

“I am the Ghost of Christmas Past,” the figure told Scrooge.

Because the figure was so small, Scrooge just laughed at him.

“Silence!” ordered the Spirit. “You must come with me. Catch hold of my coattails, and we will travel to a time when Christmas was not a humbug!”

Scrooge hesitated, but he remembered Marley’s
warning and took hold of the Spirit’s coat.
