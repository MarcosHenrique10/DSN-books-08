In the next window Scrooge peered through, he saw himself, many years past. He sat behind a desk covered in money, scowling at a crying Isabelle.

Scrooge remembered how he had taken Isabelle’s home from her when she could not pay her bills.

“Your greed drove her away,” said the Spirit. “You were to marry, but you loved only money.”

“No . . .” Scrooge moaned, “I loved Isabelle. I cannot bear these memories. Take me home!”
