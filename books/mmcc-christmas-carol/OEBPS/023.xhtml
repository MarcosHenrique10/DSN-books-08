The window flew open, and Scrooge and the Spirit of Christmas Past soared through the night and over the rooftops. They stopped at a brightly lit house. When Scrooge looked inside, he couldn’t believe his eyes. It was Isabelle, the girl he’d once loved, happily dancing with someone.

“Who is that?” he asked.

“That man was you, Scrooge,” said the Spirit, “in the days when you were kind and cheerful.” Then the Spirit whisked Scrooge away again.
