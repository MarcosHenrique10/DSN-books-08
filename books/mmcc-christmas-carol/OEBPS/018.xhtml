“It is I, Jacob Marley,” the ghost declared. “And these burdens I bear, heavier than all the treasure in the world, are my miserable deeds. They are chained to me forever. I have come to warn you: change your ways, or you are doomed to be like me!”

“You will be visited tonight by three spirits,” the ghost continued. “Listen to them well, for only they can save you now.”

Then the ghost of Jacob Marley disappeared!
