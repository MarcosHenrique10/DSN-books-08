Out in the backyard, poor Pluto was still stuck under
 the sheet. He couldn’t see to find his doggy door.

Hearing his whimpers, Daisy looked around. “What’s
 that sound?” she asked.

Just then, something weird and white ran past the
 window.

Goofy gasped. “It’s a g-g-ghost!” he said.

Mickey’s friends were too scared even to scream!
