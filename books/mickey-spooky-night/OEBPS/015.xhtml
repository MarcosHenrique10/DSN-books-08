As the thunder boomed and the lightning flashed,
 Minnie and her friends raced into Mickey’s house. “Mickey,
 we’re here!” Minnie called.

But there was no answer. Mickey was too far upstairs
 to hear anything over the sounds of the storm.

Just then, the lights went out! Mickey’s friends heard
 weird, thumping sounds above them.

Minnie gasped. “What was that?” she asked.
