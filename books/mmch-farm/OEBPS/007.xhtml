“Whoa, ya tricky little tractor!”

Goofy doesn’t know how to stop the runaway clickety-clack tractor!

“Step aside, everyone!” shouts Donald. He throws his lasso over the
tractor’s red lever and pulls. It works! The tractor comes to a halt.

“Sorry I goofed up, Mickey,” says Goofy.

Mickey smiles. “That’s okay, Goof. You took us right where I wanted to go!”
