As King Triton lifted the unconscious Ariel into his arms, the
music box fell from her hand and began to play. King Triton
remembered how happy his family used to be. He softly sang the
queen’s lullaby to his daughter.

“I’m so sorry I didn’t listen,” King Triton told the Little Mermaid.

Ariel slowly opened her eyes and hugged her father. “Daddy, let’s go home.”
