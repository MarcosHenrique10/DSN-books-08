Alone, Ariel felt disappointed and frustrated. Her tail accidentally
brushed against a bass string. She loved the sound! Soon she was playing the
instruments and singing.

Flounder, Sebastian, and the band came out from hiding when they heard
the Little Mermaid’s beautiful voice. Realizing how much Ariel loved music,
Sebastian made her take the musician’s oath: “Do you promise to jump, jive,
wail, groove, rock steady, and at all times lend a helping hand to your fellow
music lovers?”

“I do!” Ariel promised.
