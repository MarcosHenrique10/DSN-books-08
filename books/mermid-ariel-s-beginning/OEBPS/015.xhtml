The next day, Ariel’s sisters found out about her secret adventure.
They wanted to go to the club, too. So that night, Ariel took them to the
Catfish Club.

The princesses were having so much fun that they didn’t notice
someone spying on them. It was Marina Del Rey— and she
couldn’t wait to report everything to King Triton. He
was furious! “I will not have music in my kingdom!”
he shouted.

King Triton destroyed the Catfish Club. Then
he had Sebastian, Flounder, and the band thrown
in jail. Just as she had planned, Marina became
the new chief of staff.
