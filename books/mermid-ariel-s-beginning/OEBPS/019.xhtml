Far from Atlantica, Ariel spotted something shining in the rocks at the
bottom of the sea. It was her mother’s music box, from so many years ago!

“When your mother died, the whole kingdom was heartbroken,”
Sebastian told Ariel. “The heart that never healed was your father’s.”

“He’s forgotten what it feels like to be happy,” said Ariel. “I have to bring
this back to him.”
