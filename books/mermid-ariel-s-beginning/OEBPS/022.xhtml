“I’ve won!” Sebastian yelled as he trapped
Marina inside a coral tube.

“This is not over!” shouted Marina. She
toppled her tube and rocketed down the hill—
straight toward the little crab!

Ariel jumped in front of her friend to protect
him, but she was knocked into a deep ravine.

Just then, King Triton arrived. Seeing his
daughter in danger, he rushed down to save her.
