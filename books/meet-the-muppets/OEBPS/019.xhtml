The gang makes its last stop in Paris, France.

Miss Piggy works at a fancy French magazine.

Piggy had not seen Kermit in years,

and she missed him.

He would not come with her to France before.

The French like to eat frog legs!

“Miss Piggy,” says Walter, “we need you!”

But she will not join them.

“No. I have a life here now,” she says sadly.
