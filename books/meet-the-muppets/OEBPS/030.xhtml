The Muppets found out they still had fans

like Gary and Mary, and they even had a new one!

Tex Richman found himself unable to resist

the humor of the crazy gang.

They did not save the studio,

but in the end it did not matter.

They were happy to have one another.

Walter had a new family in the Muppets,

and Gary was still his best friend.

Not a single thing was missing.
