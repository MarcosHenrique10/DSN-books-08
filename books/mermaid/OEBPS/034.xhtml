Disney Readers: Learn to read with Disney!
This series of eBooks, all starring popular Disney characters, is
designed to guide your child from sounding out simple words
to navigating paragraphs.

Level 1: Preschool–Kindergarten
• For the earliest reader. Features large type and simple words.

Level 2: Preschool–Grade 1
• For children who are familiar with common sight words. Features
basic vocabulary and short sentences.

Level 3: Grade 1–Grade 3
• For the independent reader. Features complex sentences and
simple plots.

Level 4: Grade 2–Grade 3
• For the confident reader. Features paragraphs, challenging words,
and involved plots.
