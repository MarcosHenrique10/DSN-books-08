Mickey saw an old broom leaning against the wall and
suddenly he knew what his first spell would be.

Mickey did what the Sorcerer always did. He pointed his
finger straight at the broom, and the broom began to move.

At once, the broom had two feet . . . then a right arm . . .
and then a left arm.

Mickey commanded the broom to pick up the buckets,
walk up the stairs, and fill the buckets with water.

At Mickey’s command, the broom brought the buckets of
water back and poured the water into the vat.
