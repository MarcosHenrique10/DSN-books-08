King Triton took Ariel’s hand and gently placed a pink pearl
inside of it. It glistened like the shining sea. “This belonged to
your mother,” he said. “She would’ve wanted you to have it.”

“And you can have my blue starfish,” said her
sister Adella. “It always brings me good luck.”

Ariel hugged her father and thanked her sisters for
their help. “I’ll see you on my wedding day!” she said
to them with happiness in her heart.
