Later, Eric found Ariel alone on the balcony. He noticed tears in
Ariel’s eyes. “What’s the matter?” he asked.

“I want a human wedding, but I just wish—I just wish that my family
could be there, too.”

“Hmm, I thought you might want that. It’s all planned—we’ll be
married on the royal ship at sea.”

“That’s perfect!” she cried. “I’ll tell them right away.”
