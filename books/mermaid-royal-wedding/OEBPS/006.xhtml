But Ariel had never been to a human wedding before.
She wasn’t sure what to do. She found Grimsby busily
preparing the guest list.

Ariel was puzzled. “Why is the list so long?”

“A wedding is for the family as much as it is for the bride
and groom,” said Grimsby.

That made Ariel think about her own family. They all lived
under the sea. Would they be able to come to her wedding?
