Ariel knew Carlotta would be the best person to ask about a
wedding dress. “What does a human wedding dress feel like?
Is it hard to walk in?”

“A human wedding dress has to be beautiful and billowy
and white!” Carlotta said, excitedly sketching one dress idea
after another. “It has to be made of the finest silks and satins!
I promise you will have no problem walking in it.”
