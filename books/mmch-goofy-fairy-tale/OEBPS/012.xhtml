Deep in the woods, Goofyfella gets lost and runs into Hansel and
Gretel Mouse. They’re lost, too.

“Maybe somebody in that house can help us,” says Goofyfella.

“No,” Gretel Mouse warns. “That’s the witch’s house!”

Just then, a voice calls, “Hello-o-o.” It’s Witch Clarabelle!

“Are you a good witch?” asks Goofyfella.

“Oh, yes,” laughs Witch Clarabelle. “I’m not scary at all.”
