Toodles’s cousin, Goofles, comes to help with four Mouseketools—suction
cups, sticky tape, an oar, and a Mystery Mouseketool. Goofyfella chooses
the tape to fix the flute. It works!

When Pied Piper Donald plays the new song, the ducklings line up
and follow him. “Thanks, Goofyfella!” says Pied Piper Donald.

“You’re welcome,” says Goofyfella. “How do I get to the castle?”

“Just go through the woods,” says Pied Piper Donald. “Bye-bye!”
