Prince Pete’s library is stacked high with books.

“What does the gold storybook look like?” he asks.

“It’s decorated with three shapes,” says Goofyfella.

“A heart, a diamond, and a rose.”

“I see it!” shouts Daisy Beauty. “But it’s up so high.”

“I know just what to do,” says Goofyfella.

“Oh, Goofles!”
