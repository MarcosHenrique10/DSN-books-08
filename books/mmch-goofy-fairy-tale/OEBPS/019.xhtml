Beast Pete is delighted. “There will be music and dancing,” he says.
“And I’ll invite everyone to come!” Beast Pete turns to Daisy Beauty.

“Will you be my guest?” he politely asks.

Daisy Beauty sees that Beast Pete is really very kind.

“Yes,” she answers. “We can be friends, too.”

All of a sudden, Beast Pete turns into a prince. Daisy Beauty’s friendship
broke the spell!
