Oops! Goofy slips and falls
through the magic door. A wall
of glass slams shut!

 

“Hoo-boy,” says the professor. “My remote control went kaput. Goofy’s stuck.”

“Oh, dear,” Daisy says. “Goofy can’t get the gold storybook by himself.”

“He’ll just goof up,” adds Donald.

“I’m sure you can do it, pal,” Mickey tells Goofy. “Good luck!”
