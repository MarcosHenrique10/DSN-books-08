Beast Pete comes to the door. “What do you want?” he roars.

“We want you to let Daisy Beauty go!” says Chip Thumb.

“It’s not nice to keep her locked inside,” says Goofyfella.

“I know,” Beast Pete admits. “I just wanted a friend.”

“Well, let’s have a party,” Goofyfella says. “That’s a good way
to make friends.”
