Then Dr. Daisy held up a needle. When Pluto saw it, he
covered his head.

“Pluto’s never liked shots,” Mickey told Dr. Daisy. Just then
he had an idea. He knew something Pluto did like.

Mickey reached into his pocket and pulled out a treat. “How
about a bone, Pluto?” he asked.

Pluto quickly sat up. He was so focused on the bone that he
didn’t even notice when Dr. Daisy gave him his shot!
