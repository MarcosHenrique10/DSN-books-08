Dr. Daisy scratched Pluto’s back. “I can already tell you’re
going to be a good patient,” she said.

Dr. Daisy looked in Pluto’s ears to make sure they were nice
and clean. She checked his teeth to make sure they were healthy.
She even weighed him and measured his height.

By the time Dr. Daisy listened to Pluto’s heart, Pluto was
starting to think that getting a checkup was a lot of fun!
