It was the day before Christmas. Mickey’s
coat was old and tattered. His Christmas tree was small. His
pockets were as empty as the stockings hanging in homes all
over town.

But Mickey was happy as he strolled with Pluto down the
street looking for the perfect gift for Minnie.

Suddenly, Pluto started to bark outside of a shop window.
Mickey looked inside and saw a beautiful gold necklace.

“There it is, Pluto,” Mickey sighed. “The perfect gift to
go with Minnie’s watch!”
