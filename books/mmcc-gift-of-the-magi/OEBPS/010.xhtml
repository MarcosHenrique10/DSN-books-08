Mickey asked Minnie what time it was.

“Let’s see,” she replied, pulling a string out of her pocket.
On the end of the string hung a lovely old watch.

“That sure is beautiful,” Mickey said. “I’ll bet it would
look lovely on a gold chain around your neck.” Mickey winked
at Pluto, remembering the present he hoped to buy her.

Then Minnie realized what time it was . . . she had to get
to work!

Mickey and Pluto decided to drop her off at work—in
style!
