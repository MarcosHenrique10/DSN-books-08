The people in charge of the toy drive asked Mickey if
he’d play for the crowd, and Mickey happily agreed. Soon his
music was delighting everyone. The crowd was so moved that
they donated lots of toys.

“You and that harmonica sure make a great team,” the
fire chief told Mickey.

“Yup,” Mickey agreed. “She’s worth her weight in gold,
all right!”

Mickey’s eyes lit up. “That’s it!” he shouted. Mickey
looked at the clock. “Come on, Pluto! We still have two
minutes to get to the shop!”
