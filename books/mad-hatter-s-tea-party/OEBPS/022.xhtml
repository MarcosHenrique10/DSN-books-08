“Why, there seems to be lots of room,” Alice said.
 “I heard singing, and it sounded so delightful—”

“It did?” cried the Mad Hatter. “What a charming
 child. Come in, my dear. Sit down, sit down.”

“Whose birthday is it?” Alice asked as she sat in an
 empty chair.

“No one’s. It’s an un-birthday party,” they said,
 and explained.

“Why, then it’s my un-birthday, too,” Alice said.

“A very merry un-birthday to you!” sang the Mad
 Hatter and the March Hare.
