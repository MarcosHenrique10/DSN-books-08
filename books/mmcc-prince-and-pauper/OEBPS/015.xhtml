The prince ran to the window. Below him, Captain Pete
had caught Mickey and Pluto.

“Captain!” the prince shouted. “My father needs his
peace and quiet!”

The Captain said Mickey and Pluto were just riffraff,
but the prince told him that all subjects deserve respect,
and wanted to meet them.

“As you wish!” Captain Pete replied. When the prince
turned away, Captain Pete kicked Mickey through the air.
The guard scooped Mickey up and shoved him into the
castle, shutting the door behind him.
