Meanwhile, inside the dungeon, a dark shadow slid
toward the prince’s cell. He was holding an ax and wore a
hood over his eyes. The executioner!

He took a step forward, and then another. Just then, he
tripped over a bucket! The ax flew out of his hand and landed inches above the guard’s head.

“Hyuck— sorry,” came the voice beneath the hood.

As the executioner pried the ax out of the wall, the handle
came down on the guard’s head with a THWACK!
