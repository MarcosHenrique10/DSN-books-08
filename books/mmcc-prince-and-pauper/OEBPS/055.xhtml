In the coronation hall, time was running out. Unless Mickey did
something soon, he would be crowned king and Captain Pete would
continue to steal from his subjects.

The archbishop was about to place the crown on Mickey’s head
when Captain Pete and his guard raced into the room.

“STOP!” Mickey shouted, and ordered the guards
to seize Captain Pete.

“Seize him!” said Pete, pointing to Mickey.
“He’s an imposter.”
