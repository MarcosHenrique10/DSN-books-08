“What a great idea!” he said. “I’ll take your place in the
streets, and you will be a prince!”

Mickey didn’t like that idea. He didn’t know how to
be a prince. But the prince said it was easy— all Mickey
would have to say was “Splendid!” and “Guards, seize him!”
Besides, it would only be for a day.

The prince and Mickey traded clothes, and the prince
climbed out of the window, careful to avoid Captain Pete.
