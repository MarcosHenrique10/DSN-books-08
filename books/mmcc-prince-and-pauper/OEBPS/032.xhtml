Back in the village, the prince emerged from the alley to find
himself in the middle of a fight. A peasant lady was trying to stop
Pete’s henchmen from stealing her last chicken.

“Halt!” the prince cried, and commanded the guards to let the
chicken go.

They took one look at him and laughed. “I think you forgot yer
crown,” one guard said, then smashed a pumpkin onto the prince’s
head.

“I can’t believe it,” said the prince. “Stealing in the king’s name!”
