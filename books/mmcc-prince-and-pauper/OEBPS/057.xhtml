But before the guards could seize anyone, a regal voice declared, “He may be an imposter, but I’m not, Captain!”

All eyes turned to the ceiling, where the prince leaped from a perch, grabbed hold of the chandelier, and flew down to the ground.

“W- w- wait a minute, Your Majesty,” said Pete. “I can
explain everything.”

“Very well, go ahead,” said the prince.

But instead of speaking, Pete struck at the prince with his
sword! The prince blocked the blow with his own sword. He
sliced at Pete’s pants, which fell down around the captain’s
ankles. The crowd laughed as
Captain Pete ran from
the castle.
