Goofy did his best to defend the prince, but this time it
wasn’t enough. The guards surrounded the prince and forced
him out the door.

The guards threw the prince into the castle dungeon
with Donald. “Your Highness, we’re saved!” Donald cried,
throwing his arms around the prince.

But the door to the dungeon clanged shut. “Wait a
minute,” Donald cried. “They’ve captured you, too? We’re
doomed!”
