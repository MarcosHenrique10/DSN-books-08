The prince was still standing in the street when a big
open wagon filled with food came clattering down the street.
“Make way for the royal provisioner!” the driver shouted.

The townspeople ran, but the prince jumped directly in
front of the oncoming horse. He held up his royal ring and
commanded the driver to surrender the food.

The driver gasped and stopped the wagon as the crowd
knelt before the prince. The prince climbed aboard and
tossed food down to the peasants.
