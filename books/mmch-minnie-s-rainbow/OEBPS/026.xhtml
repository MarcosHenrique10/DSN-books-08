You can make a rainbow right on your wall! What colors
do you see?

You need:
• A small mirror
• A clear jar filled with water
• A flashlight
• A white wall

How it works:

1. Place the mirror in the jar.
2. Bring the jar into a dark room with white walls.
3. Turn on the flashlight and shine the light onto the mirror.
4. If you don’t see a rainbow, try changing the angle of the flashlight until you do.
