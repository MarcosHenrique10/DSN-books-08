Hooray! The Clubhouse was almost back together, but one piece

was still missing.

“Where’s the Glove Balloon!” cried Mickey. “I put all our Easter eggs in it!”

Just then, Pete walked over. “The Glove Balloon floated up to the Giant’s

cloud. But I’ll help you get it. I feel bad that I made the Clubhouse go all

to pieces.”

So Mickey and Pete used the Mystery Mousketool—a bunch of balloons—

to float up to the cloud.
