Pete disguised himself as Mickey and knocked on the Clubhouse door.

“Hi, Minnie!” Pete said in a high-pitched voice. “It’s me, Mickey,

back with the cake!”

Minnie knew something was strange. “What are the magic

Clubhouse words then?”

“Uh…Peeska! Pooska! Pete the Cat…” Pete stammered.

But those were not the magic words. Suddenly, the Clubhouse

started to come apart and pieces floated away!
